Formlabs Mold 1

Role
Designer / Capstone Project
Tools
Fusion 360, Adobe Suite, Form 3, Bambu P1S
Year
2026

Overview

The Formlabs Mold 1 is a concept desktop injection molder designed as a natural extension of Formlabs' existing product ecosystem. The goal was to take low-volume injection molding and package it at desktop scale without losing the precision a real production tool need. This was my capstone project for my Industrial Design degree at the University of Louisiana at Lafayette.

Design brief

Formlabs makes additive manufacturing machines that can be applied to a variety of industries. Their resin printers are great for rapid prototypes, and their SLS machines are excellent for near final models, but they don’t fully replicate the material properties or surface finish of an injection-molded part. With countless small businesses starting from the growth of affordable and approachable FDM machines, the demand for approachable injection molders with custom molds is present. The brief I set for myself: design a functional desktop injection molded the way Formlabs would design this machine.

Process

I did not originally design with the intent of using Formlabs branding. I created a looks like model that represents a clamping system with a spool holder and separate nozzle that would be attached to the top. The intend was to use preexisting 3d printing parts and systems and adapt them to injection molding by pushing a spool of plastic filament at a rapid speed through the nozzle into the mold. However, this would require additional heating components to warm the mold and keep the plastic from clogging at the top. It would also require a nozzle to reach a dangerously high temperature to get a majority of plastics in a liquid state.

This was the point wear I began focusing my sketches on a dedicated injection molding machine using traditional injection molding layouts.
