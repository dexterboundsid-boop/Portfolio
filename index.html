About

Hello there, my name is Dexter Bounds, and I am currently a student at the University of Louisiana at Lafayette seeking a major in Industrial Design and have a minor in Moving Image Arts. I aspire to create fun and interactive products that focus on curiosity and encourage engagement from the user.

I have been working with 3D printers since i was 15 and use these machines for a wide variety of projects with si-fi armor and helmets being the largest scale. I have visited all 50 states while living for 2+ years in Arkansas, Ohio, Flordia and Louisiana while also living 2 1/2 years in Tianjing, China. I also work in my university’s digital fabrication lab where I teach students across multiple majors how to use and design to be made on 3d printers and laser cutters.
